Aptly — AI Interview Coach That Watches, Listens & Corrects Problem Statement Placement season exposes a brutal truth: students from tier-2/3 colleges lose offers on communication, not knowledge. Filler words when unsure, monotone delivery, no answer structure, eyes wandering offcamera in virtual interviews. The fix — dozens of mock interviews with specific feedback — requires human coaches nobody can book at scale. Speech recognition, vision models, and LLMs together can deliver 80% of that coaching for free. Build an AI mock-interview platform that conducts a realistic voice interview on camera and returns feedback on both dimensions: what was said (content) and how it was said (delivery), specific enough to act on the same day. Dependencies • Depends on browser-based audio/video capture (WebRTC/MediaRecorder) for the live interview. • Depends on speech-to-text with timestamps (Whisper) for filler-word and pacing analysis. • Depends on computer-vision gaze/head-pose tracking (MediaPipe Face Mesh) for eye-contact estimation. • Depends on an LLM for dynamic, role-aware question generation and answer-grounded follow-up questions — a fixed question list does not satisfy this dependency. • Depends on a text-to-speech voice for the AI interviewer. • Depends on a live 10-minute on-stage interview with a judge as the core validation. Minimum Requirements • Role-aware dynamic interview: user pastes a job description → AI generates relevant questions, asks them with voice, and asks follow-ups based on the actual answer given. • Content analysis per answer: relevance, structure (STAR detection for behavioural answers), technical depth, and unsupported claims, with a written note per answer. • Delivery analysis from the recording: filler-word count with timestamps of where they spiked, speaking pace (WPM) vs. an ideal band, dead-pause pattern, eye-contact estimate and look-away moments from the webcam, voice-energy trend. • Report card: per-question feedback + overall scores + the 3 most damaging habits, each paired with one concrete practice drill. • Stretch goals (beyond the minimum, for higher scoring): Progress tracking across sessions — show measurable improvement graphs. Panel mode: two AI interviewer personas (friendly HR + skeptical tech lead). Confidence-trend detection: where in the interview did the candidate crumble? • Deliverables required alongside the working demo: Working platform + full live interview with a judge. Generated report reviewed on stage against the actual recording. Delivery-metric validation: show your filler/pace/gaze numbers are real (spot-check against the video). Privacy note: where video is processed, where stored, when deleted. • Judging criteria and weightage: Interview realism (25%) — dynamic follow-ups, natural voice flow. Delivery-analysis accuracy (25%) — metrics spot-checked against the recording. Feedback specificity (25%) — actionable, evidence-backed, drill-paired. Content evaluation quality (15%) — STAR/depth/relevance judgments hold up. Privacy & polish (10%) — data handling, UX quality. • Suggested kit & stack: Browser recording (WebRTC/MediaRecorder) · Whisper for transcription with timestamps · MediaPipe Face Mesh for gaze/head pose · LLM for question generation, followups, and content scoring · TTS for the interviewer voice · report dashboard.
